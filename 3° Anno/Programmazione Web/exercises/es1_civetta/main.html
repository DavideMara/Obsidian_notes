Drink Menù

Mojito: A traditional Cuban punch. The cocktail consists of five ingredients: white rum, sugar (sugar cane juice), lime juice, soda water, and mint

Negroni: A traditional Italian drink. The cocktail consist of a made of equal parts gin, vermouth rosso (red, semi-sweet), and Campari, served with ice, and garnished with an orange slice.
